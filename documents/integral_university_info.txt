# SECTION 4: SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID

Official source:
https://www.iul.ac.in/admissioninfo/Integral_University_Scholarships_Schemes.aspx

The university scholarship page lists the following schemes and conditions:

1. Merit-Based Scholarship
   - At least 90% marks: 100% tuition-fee waiver.
   - At least 85% marks: 50% tuition-fee waiver.
   - At least 80% marks: 25% tuition-fee waiver.

2. Sibling Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for a sibling of an enrolled student.

3. Alumni / Alumni Sibling Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible alumni or an alumnus's sibling, as described on the official page.

4. Staff Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for an eligible child or spouse of a university employee.

5. Jammu and Kashmir Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants from Jammu and Kashmir.

6. North Eastern Region Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants from India's North Eastern Region.

7. Progression Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible students taking admission in a consecutive session in Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. programs.

8. Specially Abled Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 50% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants with at least 40% disability certified by a competent medical authority.

9. Adopted Village Scholarship
   - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants from villages adopted by Integral University. The page states that it is applicable to girls only.

10. Orphan Scholarship
    - The listed scheme provides a 50% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants whose both parents are no longer alive.

11. Sports Scholarship
    - The listed scheme provides a 25% tuition-fee waiver for eligible applicants who have Uttar Pradesh domicile and the specified recognition from the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India.

12. Means-Based Scholarship
    - Financial assistance is listed for deserving students from financially underprivileged backgrounds.

13. Integral Research Fellowship (IRF)
    - The official page lists ₹6,000 per month for candidates securing first rank in the Ph.D. entrance test in each discipline.
